📋 Situation Briefing

Threat

The FBI has detected bombs in 3 places around the world at the same time. A biological attack, cyber warfare, nuclear risk and worldwide chaos are about to hit all at once.

Your Mission

Jackson, you are the bomb squad. Each bomb has many wires and only one is correct. Cut the right wire before time runs out to defuse the bomb.

Levels & Odds

  • Level 1: 5 wires · Level 2: 10 wires · Level 3: 15 wires
  • Only one wire is correct. Guessing blindly is a 1/5 chance on level 1, then 1/10, then 1/15.

Reward

You earn $1,000,000 for every bomb you defuse.

Gear

  • Extra life $500,000: survive one wrong cut
  • Cut one wire $200,000: cuts one wrong wire for you
  • +20 seconds $400,000: adds time to the clock
  • Spending money lowers your final total.

Warning

If you cut a wrong wire or time runs out, the bomb explodes. Stay calm and choose carefully.
